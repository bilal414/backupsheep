# BackupSheep Brand Identity

This directory is the canonical source for BackupSheep brand strategy, identity rules, messaging, visual-system guidance, and rollout planning.

## Strategic lock

- **Brand idea:** Own the way back.
- **Category:** The open-source backup and recovery control plane.
- **Product promise:** Backups you control. Restores you can trust.
- **Brand essence:** Quietly vigilant.
- **Archetype:** Guardian × Engineer.
- **Primary audience:** Developers, infrastructure operators, and small DevOps teams responsible for mixed web infrastructure.
- **Secondary audience:** Agencies, hosting operators, and MSPs managing multiple client environments.
- **Selected creative direction:** The Sentinel Sheep.
- **Creative balance:** 70% operational confidence, 30% memorable personality.

## Status

The strategy and identity specification in this directory are considered the working brand source of truth. Visual logo and mascot master artwork should be produced from the selected Sentinel concept and added under `brand/assets/` before replacing production logo files.

No existing production assets should be deleted until the replacement masters have been reviewed at favicon, sidebar, README, documentation, and large marketing sizes.
