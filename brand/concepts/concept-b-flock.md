# Concept B — The Flock

**Status: Explored alternative; not selected as master direction**

## Idea

Treat every protected website, database, server, and cloud resource as part of a coordinated flock. BackupSheep becomes the system that gives distributed resources one operating model.

## Visual language

- clusters of simple nodes;
- resources moving toward several storage destinations;
- grouping and permission boundaries;
- repeated rounded forms inspired subtly by wool;
- diagrams that emphasize orchestration and coverage.

## Strengths

- Excellent fit for multi-resource and multi-client operations.
- Creates a flexible pattern system for website and documentation.
- Makes team/group functionality visually intuitive.
- Scales well into architecture diagrams.

## Weaknesses

- The central brand symbol can become too abstract.
- Network-node graphics are common in infrastructure software.
- The sheep/name connection becomes weaker.
- Risks positioning BackupSheep as monitoring/orchestration software rather than backup and recovery.

## Decision

Retain “flock” as a supporting visual/community metaphor, but do not make it the primary identity. The selected Sentinel concept can use flock patterns for groups of protected resources.
