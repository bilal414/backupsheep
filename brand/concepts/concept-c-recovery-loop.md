# Concept C — The Recovery Loop

**Status: Explored alternative; not selected as master direction**

## Idea

Build the identity around return, history, reversibility, and a visible path back. The sheep appears only as subtle negative space inside an abstract recovery loop.

## Visual language

- circular or returning paths;
- layered history states;
- archive rings;
- directional paths from source to copy to recovery;
- precise technical geometry.

## Strengths

- Serious and infrastructure-first.
- Naturally expresses recovery rather than generic backup storage.
- Works well for motion and product diagrams.
- Can feel highly polished in enterprise contexts.

## Weaknesses

- Circular arrows are extremely common in backup and sync categories.
- Hiding the sheep sacrifices a major source of brand memorability.
- Risks becoming indistinguishable from generic continuity, sync, or observability software.

## Decision

Do not use Recovery Loop as the master identity. Reuse its strongest idea—**a visible return path**—inside Sentinel illustrations, motion, diagrams, and the “Own the way back” campaign system.
